What the process is

The order is deliberate: sitemap before page outlines, outlines before wireframes, blocks before design, specification before build. Each stage narrows uncertainty before the next begins, so design becomes the application of a system to a known structure rather than the place where the project gets figured out.

Three routes — light, standard and full — mean it scales down. Steps are omitted deliberately and the reason is recorded, which is what makes an omission defensible rather than an oversight.

Templates

Ten document templates and one workbook containing every tracker. Generated from the source scripts in build/ — regenerate them rather than editing the binaries, so the process and the paperwork cannot drift apart.

    Before you present this

    Four things are deliberately left for you to decide:

    • The commercial basis in data/assumptions.js is real (£75/hr, £8,000 average project, 25 projects a year). The leak hours and process overhead are still estimates — the trial exists to replace them with measurements.
    • The per-step effort bands in data/steps.js are a starting calibration. Record actuals on three projects and correct them.
    • Halcyon Thermal, the worked example, is invented. Swap it for a real sanitised project if you would rather the guide referenced actual work.
    • Everything tagged New is an authored addition. Review and reject anything you disagree with — the tags exist so nothing was smuggled in.
